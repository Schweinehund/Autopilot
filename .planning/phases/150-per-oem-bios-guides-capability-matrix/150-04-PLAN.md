## Cell vocabulary (D-19) — the rule the whole matrix turns on

Three permitted forms, and nothing else:

1. **A sourced value** — a short literal string stating the fact.
2. **`Not documented by vendor`** — a documented silence: the vendor's pages were checked and are
   silent.
3. **`n/a`** — a structural absence: the capability does not exist for that vendor to document.

No plus-notation, no symbols, no footnote-only cells. Collapsing a documented silence into `n/a` is
the drift Success Criterion 1 exists to prevent. Evidence pins for the silence cells live in
`## Source Attribution`, outside the tables, which keeps cells literal (D-06, D-19).

Two cells are worth naming explicitly because the research changed them:

- **Lenovo, lost supervisor password** — a **sourced value**, not `Not documented by vendor`. The
  research closed U-1 with a correction: Lenovo's own KB `ht036206` documents system-board replacement
  by a Lenovo Service Provider, with no software reset. This is a documented destructive path.
- **Lenovo, lost certificate private key** and **HP, lost Endorsement Key** — `Not documented by
  vendor`. These two are the phase's genuine documented silences and each carries a pin in
  `## Source Attribution` naming the pages checked and the date.
