<objective>
Capture the three-axis terminal re-audit at one shared commit: every workflow dispatched and read at
job level, a fresh full-depth clone reproduction, and an independent zero-context reproduction.

Purpose: this is the criterion the milestone closes on. Its two known failure modes are reading a
top-level run colour instead of job-level output, and gathering the three axes at different commits.
Both are designed out here rather than watched for.

Output: an evidence section with a per-workflow, per-job table keyed on four fields, an explicit skip
classification, and two reproduction results at the same commit.
</objective>

  re-checking and is wrong.
- **Axis two** — every workflow dispatched on the default branch at the recorded commit, read at job
  level.
- **Axis three** — an independent zero-context reproduction against the working tree, barred from
  reading any planning document, at the same recorded commit.

All three are keyed to the single commit produced by the owner push. If that commit changes, all three
must be re-run.
</axis_definitions>
