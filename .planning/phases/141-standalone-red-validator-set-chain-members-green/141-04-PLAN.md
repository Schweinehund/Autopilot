<objective>
Repair the two timeout root causes and enable the CI fan-out, in the ratified order: the
in-script chain-spawn caps first, then the workflow layer — both strictly before Plan 06's
dispatched run, so the fan-out reds on content rather than on wall clock and so a green fan-out
cannot be confused with an unrun one.

Purpose: per D-16 the draft's verdict that this phase carries no timeout root-cause class is
false twice over — it rested on a contaminated measurement and never checked CI.
`check-phase-66`'s chain spawn runs a ~305-second child under a 300-second cap, and on
timeout the catch cannot classify the error, so it returns a failure indistinguishable from a
real one. Separately, four PR-blocking CI jobs carry 15-minute caps against a floor that expands
through the whole 48-to-66 sub-chain, because their peer short-circuit covers only phases 67 and
above. And while every fanned-out job is gated on the upstream harness succeeding, 19 of them
have reported skipped on every ref all milestone.

Output: two raised script caps, 29 always-evaluating job conditions, four raised job caps, five
ledger rows, and one measured bare `check-phase-68` figure.
