## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 — plan self-inconsistency] The `RE-index.md` added-line count is 21, not the 12-or-13 the acceptance criterion predicted**

- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** The acceptance criterion reads "12 or 13 added lines (eleven rows plus the Review
  Notes bullet, which may wrap)", which assumes a one- or two-line bullet. Task 2 Step B
  simultaneously mandates four distinct statements in the bullet and mandates matching the
  `RE-212` entry's shape, which is a nine-line wrapped bullet at this file's ~100-character width.
  The two instructions cannot both hold at 12-13 lines.
- **Resolution:** The substantive instruction governs. The bullet carries all four mandated
  statements at the `RE-212` shape and indentation, costing 10 lines (a blank separator plus a
  nine-line bullet) on top of the eleven rows. Total 21 added.
- **Load-bearing half preserved:** deleted lines is **0**, which is the assertion that actually
  guards against a rewrite or a reordering. Eleven of the 21 added lines match `^| RE-`, verified
  independently.

**2. [Rule 3 — comment-syntax conflict] The `build-publish-bundle.mjs` provenance entry is a new self-contained parenthetical rather than a continuation of the prior one**

- **Found during:** Task 3, Step 6
- **Issue:** In `build-filename-map.mjs` the Phase 137 history line ends in `;` and the comment
  block's closing `)` lives on a separate trailing line, so a Phase 152 continuation line inserts
  cleanly. In `build-publish-bundle.mjs` the Phase 137 line **carries the closing `)` itself**.
  Appending a continuation beneath it would leave the parenthetical closed one line early;
  converting that `)` to `;` would be an in-place edit of a prior history line, which D-18 and the
  plan's own prohibition bar outright.
- **Resolution:** The Phase 152 entry was written as its own balanced parenthetical at the same
  indent as the block's opening `(bumped` line. Not one byte of prior history changed —
  `grep -c 'bumped 223 -> 225 in Phase 137'` still returns 1 in both files.
