authored actually reach the publish bundle — and that the nine operations documents get there
without a document identifier and without being C17-gated.

row; it never ran the bundle, never exercised the filename-map coverage or parity checks, and never
touched the manifest — which is exactly what the ROADMAP research flag names. This plan is the
discharge.

Output: a version-tagged zip under `dist/`, a captured manifest, and a recorded measurement set.
No commit.
</objective>
