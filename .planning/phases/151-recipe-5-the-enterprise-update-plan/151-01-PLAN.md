### The commit contract (D-52) — TWO commits, not five

Plan execution on this repository is sequential on the main tree (`.planning/config.json` sets
`use_worktrees:false`), so a file authored uncommitted by one plan is still on disk when the next
plan runs.

1. **Commit A — the amendments commit**, landed by **Plan 01 Task 1**:
   `docs/_standards/EEE-SOP-standard.md` and `docs/_templates/recipe-template.md`, together, alone.
2. **Commit B — the content commit**, landed by **Plan 05**:
   `docs/recipes/05-enterprise-update-plan.md`, the two retrofitted recipes
   (`01-shared-windows-avd-client.md`, `02-shared-ipad-full-provisioning.md`), and
   `.planning/milestones/v1.19-DEFERRED-CLEANUP.md`, together, in one commit.

**Therefore plans 01 (Task 2 onward), 02, 03 and 04 author content and DO NOT COMMIT it.** They
leave the working tree dirty on purpose. **Do not fall back to a per-task commit default — a
per-task commit here violates D-52 and lands the recipe in the same commit as the template.**

Why intermediate content commits are forbidden: the link checker has no baseline, allowlist, ratchet
or expected-failure list, so a half-authored recipe whose Verification and See Also links are not yet
written, and whose Configuration-Caused-Failures Runbook cells point at anchors later plans create,
is red in every possible ordering. D-52's two-commit design makes that red state short and
uncommitted rather than pretending an ordering exists that avoids it.
