tables and one thin narrative, and prove the record is not stale.

Two independent populations are closed here. The meaning checker's findings at the recorded base
revision get a sibling disposition file, so the corpus-meaning guarantee is zero undispositioned corpus
findings rather than a process exit code the checker cannot produce. The style checker's ninety-four
