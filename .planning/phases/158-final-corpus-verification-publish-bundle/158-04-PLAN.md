<objective>
Put the two modal-changed hunks in front of a judge that has not seen this milestone's reasoning, and
record what it says without editing it.

The whole point of this step is independence. The gate whose docstring explains why the deletion
happened, the requirement text that states the same rationale in the same words, and every disposition
and verdict artifact this phase produces are all answer keys — a judge given any of them is grading its
own author's homework. The blocklist is therefore the load-bearing part of the prompt, not a formality.

Ordering: D-69 constraint 3 puts the judge after the sweep, which landed in plan 158-03, so the judge
sees the corpus in the state the bundle will ship. The packet emitter's fix landed in the same plan, so
the packets carry the region key the prompt now enumerates.

Purpose: satisfy the milestone's modal-loss judgment requirement on this phase's slice with a fresh
process and a captured transcript, and record honestly what that judgment does not cover.
Output: the committed prompt, the captured raw output, the verdict record with its hashes, and the
findings narrative with its blindness statement.
</objective>
