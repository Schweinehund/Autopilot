<objective>
Resolve the two external claims against source bytes, then land the six reconciliation corrections
and the licensing propagation they authorize.

Purpose: RCP-07(c) and RCP-08 both state a mechanism — that a self-deploying kiosk never triggers
per-user subscription activation and that the `ShellLauncher` node therefore fails on an unactivated
Enterprise image — as established fact, and no requirement records that both rest on the same
unresolved external claim. RESEARCH.md fetched Microsoft's own AssignedAccess CSP reference and found
the gate stated in edition-only terms, with a corroborating real-world report of the identical error
string on Windows 11 Pro, which is an edition failure and not an activation failure. That resolution
changes what three documents may say. It is re-confirmed here before it is spent.

Output: a committed claims artifact recording both dispositions with their fetched evidence, three
reconciled corrections in RE-222, three in RE-224, the licensing consequence propagated to
`docs/reference/licensing-matrix.md`, and all three documents re-stamped.
</objective>
