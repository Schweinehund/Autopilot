# Requirements: v1.22 Google-Style Corpus Landing, Authoring-Standard Adoption & Walk-Up AVD Kiosk Recipe

**Defined:** 2026-08-31
**Core Value:** IT teams can independently provision, troubleshoot, and manage Windows, macOS, iOS/iPadOS, Android, and Linux devices through Microsoft Intune / Entra ID without escalating to engineering — and find those answers as clean, correctly-cited results in the Copilot Studio / SharePoint knowledge base. v1.22 makes that corpus read as one voice: a single adopted writing standard applied to every document, proven not to have changed any document's meaning, and carried forward into how new documents are authored.

**Milestone bar:** the verified Google-style pass is landed in `docs/` and extended to every document authored since it forked, with the deterministic verifier at zero and a fresh-agent judge verdict recorded for all new material; the walk-up AVD kiosk build is a registered, reconciled, publish-reachable Recipe #6 with its executable assets committed; the style skill is wired into document creation with a gate bounded to what can actually report zero; and both harness debts (`V121` back-anchor pin, C17 frozen-aware residue) are discharged with the apex green at close and the result pushed and tagged.

**Scope origin.** This milestone was scoped from two live findings, not from a research pass. First, the 9-batch Google-style pass over the doc library is **already complete and already fully verified** in the scratch corpus `D:\claude\docs-google-style-test` — it was never landed because landing was an owner decision, and the corpus moved underneath it. Second, a walk-up AVD kiosk recipe and its planting seed were authored **outside the GSD phase flow** on 2026-08-31 from a live pilot build, and arrived untracked, unregistered, and carrying six unreconciled conflicts against two shipped recipes.

**Owner rulings taken at milestone start (2026-08-31), all four recorded inline below:**

1. **Landing strategy = MERGE, then batch 10.** Not a fresh re-run. Preserves the 406-hunk / 10-agent judge verification rather than repeating it against a moved corpus.
2. **"Verified end to end" = all four halves.** Deterministic slice at zero, a judge pass over new material only, the full structural gate before *and* after landing, and a rebuilt publish bundle.
3. **Recipe #6's executable assets = a new top-level `assets/recipes/06-walk-up-avd-kiosk/` tree**, outside `docs/`.
4. **Hygiene scope = the located defects only.** The corpus-wide past-due freshness population stays out.

**Evidence labels.** `[MEASURED]` = a command was run against this repo, and it is named. `[SOURCED]` = a first-party page was fetched, with its own `ms.date`/`updated_at` recorded. `[UNVERIFIED]` = could not be confirmed; **must not be written as fact in any deliverable**. `[PILOT]` is new this milestone: a behaviour observed on one device in one tenant during the 2026-08-27→30 build. `[PILOT]` is **not** `[SOURCED]` — the harness cannot validate it, and no promotion review may treat it as though it were first-party documentation.

### Standard — Google style as the authoring standard (STD)

**The gate is bounded on purpose, and this is the load-bearing decision of the pillar.** `[MEASURED 2026-08-24]` `check.py --no-headings` returns **9,363 candidates on the finished, fully human-audited corpus**: 3,324 passive (owner-ruled REPORT ONLY, permanent), 2,916 em-dash spacing, 2,573 narrow word-list entries out of scope by design, 222 Android-`admin`, 303 assorted. **No rule class is at zero.**

- [ ] **STD-01**: The `google-style` skill is wired into the document-creation flow so that any new corpus document is authored against it, and the pairing with `google-style-verify` is documented for any bulk prose rewrite.
- [ ] **STD-02**: A mechanical gate is added **only** over the slice that can actually report zero inside unprotected prose. Three classes are permanently excluded and the exclusion is recorded with its reason, not left implicit: the em-dash rule, because ten `v1.NN-milestone-audit.mjs` validators content-match the em dash used as a table N/A placeholder — **the corpus's own gate requires what the style guide bars**; the Android `admin` class, because Google's `word-list.md` self-contradicts (it says write out `administrator`, and also that `admin` is acceptable in Android documentation, and also bars `Android: administrator`); and headings, per STYLE-04.
- [ ] **STD-03**: The non-gate is written down where a future milestone will find it, as a standing rule: **never mandate a rule set whose checker cannot report zero on work already accepted.** Without this, a later milestone re-litigates the 9,363 as a backlog rather than a permanent, reasoned exclusion.
- [ ] **STD-04**: The authoring guidance records the two rewrite hazards the judge pass measured, because both invert naive intuition. **`must` is the dangerous rewrite:** promoting a hedge to `must` (26 cases) outnumbered flattening an expectation into a bare assertion (19), and the verify skill had itself *recommended* the promotion before the clause was removed. And **passive→active is the only rule that can invent a fact**, because the source sentence deliberately withheld the actor — defaulting to *you* (the reader) is the ruled resolution, and every other forced-actor choice is flagged for review rather than assumed.

## Out of Scope

Explicit boundaries with reasoning, to prevent re-adding.

- **Re-running the Google-style pass from scratch against HEAD.** Owner ruling 1. It would discard a 406-hunk, 10-agent verification to avoid a merge, and the merge is the cheaper of the two.
- **Re-judging the previously-judged 406 hunks.** They carry recorded verdicts with quoted evidence. The raw worklist still prints a larger number because the detector is deliberately over-inclusive; that number is not a work item.
- **Sentence-case headings.** Permanent fork, ratified 2026-08-23 and re-affirmed as STYLE-04. 101 headings are pinned across 83 validators and the standard's own worked example is Title Case.
- **Gating CI on `check.py`.** Structurally impossible, not merely expensive: the checker cannot report zero on a corpus already accepted, and one of its rules is in direct conflict with ten of this repository's own validators. STD-02 and STD-03 record the bounded alternative.
- **The corpus-wide freshness sweep and a `review_by`-versus-today validator.** Owner ruling 4. Milestone-sized on its own; its own trigger names a dedicated hygiene milestone.
- **Retrofitting the legacy `docs/operations/` documents to EEE / C17.** Carried from v1.21; publish reach is already available without it.
- **Editing the three do-not-edit-ruled defects.** Listed under Future Requirements with their measured falsifications intact, so the ruling is visible rather than the defects being silently forgotten.
- **Rewriting vendor documentation.** Carried constraint: recipe and firmware guides stay delivery-shaped and link-not-copy where the vendor page is authoritative.
- **A second repository for recipe assets.** Rejected at owner ruling 3; `assets/recipes/` keeps them versioned beside the document that describes them, without entering the publish walk.
