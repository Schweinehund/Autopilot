prohibitions bar any `docs/` content file being created, edited or deleted. Nothing under `docs/` is

pass (`judge-packets.py`, 406 verdicts) both ran to completion in the sessions that produced these
commits, and `google-style-verify` exists specifically to re-prove a completed pass did not change
meaning.

**What remains open.** The pass has not been **landed** against the live corpus — it is vendored
tooling and its own verdicts, not a corpus-wide rewrite applied to `docs/`. Whether and how to apply
its findings to the shipped corpus is an owner call recorded, not resolved, by this milestone (see
