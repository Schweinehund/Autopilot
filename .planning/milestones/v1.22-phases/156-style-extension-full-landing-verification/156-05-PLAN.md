The full batch-10 judge pass: regenerate packets over all thirteen documents at the phase BASE,
dispatch one fresh judge process per non-empty packet from the committed prompt's own bytes, record a
verdict with quoted evidence per hunk, apply the fixes with their rule-row deletions in the same
commit, and close with the idempotency re-sweep.

Purpose: the structural gate cannot see this phase's core risk. On the completed nine-batch pass,
thirty semantic defects shipped or nearly shipped and every one of them passed the full structural
gate; the judge half found four point six times what the deterministic half found, and its largest
class was a hedge promoted to a mandate — twenty-six against nineteen inversions. This plan is the
only step in the phase that can catch that class.

The tracer in plan 156-03 already proved every mechanism here on one document and one packet. This
plan runs the same mechanisms over the full landed batch without re-deriving them, and closes the
loop with D-05's mandatory idempotency proof, which is also D-37's answer to the fact that judge fixes
and the em-dash pass write NEW prose into documents this phase has already dispositioned.

The tracer document's hunks re-emit at this BASE with their post-fix after-lines, so its rows are
re-derived here and this run's rows are the authoritative ones. That is a deliberate, recorded
consequence of keeping the BASE fixed at the commit before the first batch-10 document commit, which
is what keeps the 406 out of range structurally rather than by a filter that could be got wrong.

STYLE-02 idempotency, VERIFY-02 adjacency (key uniqueness and cross-record collision), VERIFY-02
empty (the zero-hunk and empty-bin outcomes), VERIFY-02 ordering (sorted, reproducible rows),
VERIFY-02 concurrency (independent processes with no shared state), and VERIFY-03 (the explicit
out-of-task ask and its recorded findings). 16 in, 16 accounted for across the plan set.
