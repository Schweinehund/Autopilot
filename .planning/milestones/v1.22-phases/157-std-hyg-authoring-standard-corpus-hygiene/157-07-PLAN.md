<objective>
Establish Google style as the standing authoring standard: a new section in the corpus's own standards
document, the non-gate rule written where a future milestone will actually look, both measured rewrite
hazards recorded, and nine authoring surfaces pointed at the result.

This lands last by D-50, for a reason worth stating: it is the only C17-constrained authoring in the
phase, it is the text most likely to trip the gate that plan 04 made enforcing, and it is normative
prose about prose — the one place where getting the shape wrong is self-indicting.

Two of the four things being recorded invert naive intuition, which is why they are recorded at all.
Promoting a hedge to a mandate is the dangerous rewrite, not flattening one — 26 cases against 19 — and
the verifier skill had itself recommended the promotion before the clause was removed. And converting
passive to active is the only rule in the set that can invent a fact, because the source sentence
deliberately withheld the actor.

Purpose: make the standard findable by an author and un-relitigable by a later milestone.
Output: one new standards section, two pin-file entries, three homes for the standing rule, nine wiring
surfaces, and this plan's SUMMARY carrying the phase's first and last commit SHAs (D-48).
