**D-04 OS-split (v1.14 — extends v1.13 correction):**

- `check-phase-95.mjs` (v1.12 apex / v1.14 continuity, CHAIN `[48..94]`, 47 phases) → Linux GHA **sole-authoritative**: exits 0 nested (V-112-CHAIN-95 PASS).

**Caveat (unmeasured):** the OOM-vs-timeout flip risk at the new `[48..111]` depth is unmeasured. Growth is linear under `CHECK_PHASE_NESTED=1`, so the risk is LOW, but it is deeper than any prior milestone and has not been characterized on a memory-constrained cold Windows clone.
